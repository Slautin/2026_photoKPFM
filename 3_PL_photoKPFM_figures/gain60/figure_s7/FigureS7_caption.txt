Figure S7. Representative top-read PL spectra of the FAPbI₃–BDAPbI₄–PEA₂PbI₄ ternary library measured at the 22nd acquisition using a detector gain of 60. Spectra are shown for five selected nominal compositions (A1, F2, G6, D3, and C11), illustrating differences in the measured emission profiles across the library. Each spectrum was normalized to its maximum measured intensity for visualization. The absolute PL peak intensities were used for the screening analysis presented in the main manuscript. Composition labels represent nominal FAPbI₃/BDAPbI₄/PEA₂PbI₄ precursor percentages. No independent detector wavelength-response correction was applied.
