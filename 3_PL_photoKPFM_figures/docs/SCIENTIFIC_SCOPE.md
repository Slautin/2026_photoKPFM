# Scientific scope

- PL and photoKPFM measurements were matched by nominal composition, not by spatially registered fields of view.
- The 13 matched compositions are a small exploratory subset of the 96-well PL library.
- Reported associations are descriptive and do not establish causality.
- Steady-state PL intensity was measured in this study. TRPL and carrier-lifetime observations appear only as broader literature context.
- Surface-potential signals are not treated as calibrated work functions unless the source explicitly reports a calibrated work-function measurement.
- PL-derived emissive components are spectral-model outputs rather than structurally confirmed phase fractions.
- The bundled HDF5 file contains ternary-library photoKPFM measurements; it is not the unavailable binary-library dataset underlying author-supplied Figure 2.
- Figure 1 and Figure 2 are rendered author-supplied references with explicit scope, not computationally reproduced figures.
- Plot generators emit standalone scientific panels. Composite assembly and panel lettering are manuscript-production steps outside the analytical code.
